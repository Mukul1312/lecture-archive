Verified
A speech recogniser was run over the recording, and its words land in the same places as the transcript's. This is the placement check. Passing it rules out the transcript being laid against the wrong part of the recording. It cannot see a stretch shifted by seconds or a minute, so verified lectures can still have shifted passages.
Unverified
Aligned, but nobody has checked where the words landed. Most lectures are here. The highlighting may be right, or it may drift. Watching it is the check.
Failed the check
The recogniser's words and the transcript's words disagree about where things are. Shown so the fault can be seen and heard.
