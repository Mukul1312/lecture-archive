# Entry 4 — 08–09 July 1975 · Puri, on the eve of Ratha-yātrā


**[GGS] diary body** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. By the mercy of Gopāla, somehow these two days passed in Puri.
2. Tomorrow is Ratha-yātrā.
3. Our stay at Puri has come to an end.
4. Now our proposed plan is to return to Hyderabad because the temple president is repeatedly sending us letters to return after the Ratha-yātrā.
5. By his blessings, somehow this servant was able to come to Odisha, as well as print and distribute a magazine in Odia.
6. But O Lord… there is one thing for which I feel regret in my heart.
7. One devotee had promised to come and take me to my dear Gopāla's place.
8. I had a desire to stay there for one day and perform bhajana-kīrtana along with preaching Śrī Guru's teachings to the local devotees.
9. This was the special desire of this servant.
10. But O Lord… what is Your desire?
11. Why was he unable to come?
12. Not only that, even after sending a letter to him is not responding.
13. Why did we not receive any answer from the devotees at Gopāla's place?
14. Only You know what the real fact is.
15. May Your auspicious desire be fulfilled.
16. This servant only desires that You kindly give him the association of real friends, well-wishers and associates, so that this servant may perform Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga's service for Their pleasure.
17. Śrī Guru has instructed this servant to preach Śrī Guru Gaurāṅga's message by opening a centre and to translate his books to Odia.
18. How this will be possible is beyond this servant's imagination.
19. O Lord… may Your auspicious desire be fulfilled.

**[GGS] closing refrain** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. Oh my Lord, Gopāla… please shower Your mercy.
2. May this servant achieve Kṛṣṇa-prema along with proper devotee association, well-wishers and companions.
3. May this servant achieve success in the service of Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga.
4. Haribol
