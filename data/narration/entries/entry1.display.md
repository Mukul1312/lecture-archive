# Entry 1 — 13–27 April 1975 · Vṛndāvana: festival & sannyāsa dīkṣā


**[GGS] diary body** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. By the mercy of Gopāla, somehow these days passed in Hyderabad and Sri Vṛndāvana Dhāma.
2. To participate in the festival at Vṛndāvana, the journey began on April fifteenth, nineteen seventy five.
3. On April seventeenth, nineteen seventy five, this servant arrived in Vṛndāvana.
4. April twentieth, nineteen seventy five was Rāmanavamī Day — on this auspicious day, the deity installation festival was performed (at Śrī Kṛṣṇa-Balarāma temple established by Śrīla Guru Mahārāja in Vṛndāvana, Ramana Reti).
5. This festival continued for one week.
6. Following the divine order of Śrī Guru, this servant travelled to Vṛndāvana, especially to receive sannyāsa dīkṣā.
7. On April twenty third, nineteen seventy five, Wednesday, this servant accepted the daṇḍa (tridaṇḍa).
8. From today this servant offers his body, mind, speech, along with the self to the Lord (Śrī Kṛṣṇa).
9. Everything now belongs to Him.
10. May Prabhu (Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa) shower His transcendental knowledge, devotion and divine potency on this servant.
11. May this servant pass his life in Your divine service, pure love and transcendental bliss.

**[GGS] closing refrain** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. Oh my Lord, Gopāla… please shower Your mercy.
2. May this servant achieve Kṛṣṇa-prema along with proper devotee association, well-wishers and companions.
3. May this servant achieve success in the service of Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga.
