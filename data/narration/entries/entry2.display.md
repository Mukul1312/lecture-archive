# Entry 2 — 04 May 1975 (Sunday) · "You are the real desire tree"


**[NARRATOR] (not his voice)** _(base-model male narrator — not his voice)_
1. Although Śrī Śrīmad Gour Govinda Svāmī Mahārāja was situated in Hyderabad,
2. his heart was ever restless to fulfill the long-cherished desire of his Spiritual Master — to plant the seed of Kṛṣṇa consciousness in the sacred land of Utkal.
3. He was mostly concerned for the spiritual awakening of the village people, and anxious to serve his beloved Gopāla.
4. All those pure desires were the root cause of his inspiration to open a centre and spread the teachings of Śrīla Prabhupāda in Odisha.
5. We can see this from his diary as follows —

**[GGS] diary body** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. Oh Lord… as the Supersoul, You know the inner thoughts of this servant.
2. You are the real desire tree.
3. Whenever and whatever this servant desired, you fulfilled it; but now in this place, why is this servant not able to achieve divine love and transcendental bliss through service?
4. This servant gave up everything and came forward to take sannyāsa dīkṣā for the development of his dear worshipable Gopāla's place, to serve the devotees present there, as well as to preach in Odisha.
5. Oh Lord… by Your causeless mercy, in Vṛndāvana, this servant met Śrīla Prabhupāda, in the form of Śrī Guru.
6. In fact, His Divine Grace is the most magnanimous incarnation and also like a desire tree.
7. His Divine Grace gave shelter to this servant at his lotus feet and also offered him sannyāsa dīkṣā.
8. You know how this servant went to Odisha, and had to return from there.
9. But this servant's real place is in Odisha, and that You have spoken through Śrīla Prabhupāda's lotus mouth.
10. Still, You are testing this servant in a very strange way by creating such a strange situation, and breaking the strong determination of this servant.
11. Because of that, sometimes this servant becomes hopeless and feels whether you really do not want it?
12. Otherwise, why is this not happening?
13. Śrī Śrī Īśopaniṣada Hindi book printing work is completed, so please create a favourable environment in Odisha.
14. Let this servant be engaged in preaching Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga's message in Odisha.
15. For this service, please shower divine love, spiritual energy, and strong determination power.
16. If an ISKCON centre can be established in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, then this servant has a desire to open a rural preaching centre at Gopāla's place.
17. For this purpose, a confirmation from Ghanaśyāma Prabhu has already been received.
18. If You so desire, everything will be settled.
19. For that, this servant cannot force You, rather he simply begs for Your causeless mercy.
20. May You shower a drop of that mercy.

**[GGS] closing refrain** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. Oh my Lord, Gopāla… please shower Your mercy.
2. May this servant achieve Kṛṣṇa-prema along with proper devotee association, well-wishers and companions.
3. May this servant achieve success in the service of Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga.
4. Haribol
