### [GGS] diary body

By the mercy of Gopāla, somehow these days passed in Hyderabad and Sri Vṛndāvana Dhāma. To participate in the festival at Vṛndāvana, the journey began on 15-04-75. On 17-04-75, this servant arrived in Vṛndāvana. 20-4-75 was Rāmanavamī Day — on this auspicious day, the deity installation festival was performed (at Śrī Kṛṣṇa-Balarāma temple established by Śrīla Guru Mahārāja in Vṛndāvana, Ramana Reti). This festival continued for one week.

Following the divine order of Śrī Guru, this servant travelled to Vṛndāvana, especially to receive sannyāsa dīkṣā. On 23-4-75, Wednesday, this servant accepted the daṇḍa (tridaṇḍa). From today this servant offers his body, mind, speech, along with the self to the Lord (Śrī Kṛṣṇa). Everything now belongs to Him. May Prabhu (Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa) shower His transcendental knowledge, devotion and divine potency on this servant. May this servant pass his life in Your divine service, pure love and transcendental bliss.

## Entry 2 — 04 May 1975 (Sunday) · "You are the real desire tree"

- **Date:** `04-05-1975 (Sunday)` → `spoken_date(4,5,1975,"Sunday")` → "May fourth, nineteen seventy five, Sunday."
- **Narrator intro:** yes · **Closes with:** Gopāla refrain **+ [Haribol]**

### [NARRATOR] (not his voice)

Although Śrī Śrīmad Gour Govinda Svāmī Mahārāja was situated in Hyderabad, his heart was ever restless to fulfill the long-cherished desire of his Spiritual Master — to plant the seed of Kṛṣṇa consciousness in the sacred land of Utkal. He was mostly concerned for the spiritual awakening of the village people, and anxious to serve his beloved Gopāla. All those pure desires were the root cause of his inspiration to open a centre and spread the teachings of Śrīla Prabhupāda in Odisha. We can see this from his diary as follows —

### [GGS] diary body

Oh Lord! As the Supersoul, You know the inner thoughts of this servant. You are the real desire tree. Whenever and whatever this servant desired, you fulfilled it; but now in this place, why is this servant not able to achieve divine love and transcendental bliss through service? This servant gave up everything and came forward to take sannyāsa dīkṣā for the development of his dear worshipable Gopāla's place, to serve the devotees present there, as well as to preach in Odisha.

Oh Lord! By Your causeless mercy, in Vṛndāvana, this servant met Śrīla Prabhupāda, in the form of Śrī Guru. In fact, His Divine Grace is the most magnanimous incarnation and also like a desire tree. His Divine Grace gave shelter to this servant at his lotus feet and also offered him sannyāsa dīkṣā. You know how this servant went to Odisha, and had to return from there. But this servant's real place is in Odisha, and that You have spoken through Śrīla Prabhupāda's lotus mouth. Still, You are testing this servant in a very strange way by creating such a strange situation, and breaking the strong determination of this servant. Because of that, sometimes this servant becomes hopeless and feels whether you really do not want it? Otherwise, why is this not happening?

Śrī Śrī Īśopaniṣada Hindi book printing work is completed, so please create a favourable environment in Odisha. Let this servant be engaged in preaching Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga's message in Odisha. For this service, please shower divine love, spiritual energy, and strong determination power. If an ISKCON centre can be established in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, then this servant has a desire to open a rural preaching centre at Gopāla's place. For this purpose, a confirmation from Ghanaśyāma Prabhu has already been received. If You so desire, everything will be settled. For that, this servant cannot force You, rather he simply begs for Your causeless mercy. May You shower a drop of that mercy.

### [GGS] closing refrain

Oh my Lord, Gopāla! Please shower Your mercy. May this servant achieve Kṛṣṇa-prema along with proper devotee association, well-wishers and companions. May this servant achieve success in the service of Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga. [Haribol]

## Entry 3 — 01 June 1975 · "just like a machine in Your hands"

- **Date:** `01-06-1975` (no weekday) → `spoken_date(1,6,1975)` → "June first, nineteen seventy five."
- **Narrator intro:** yes · **Closes with:** Gopāla refrain (no Haribol)

### [NARRATOR] (not his voice)

Once again, on the following day, the remembrance of Gopāla's place awakened in the heart of His Holiness Gaur Govinda Swāmī Mahārāja. How he offered heartfelt prayers at the lotus feet of the Lord to fulfill that divine longing, can be seen from his diary as follows.

### [GGS] diary body

Oh Lord! What You are doing — only You know. This servant is unable to understand anything. This servant is just like a machine in Your hands. What You want to accomplish through this servant, You know it well.

Whatever is Your plan for this servant, You know it very well. Anyhow, this servant has a strong desire, You know about his Gopāla's place. This servant has developed a desire to open a branch of ISKCON at Gadeigiri, the place of Gopāla, to teach and preach about the Bhagavad-gītā to the devotees present there. At present, Gopāla's place is in a very wretched condition. How can we develop this place? People in general are forgetting Gopāla, how can they once again remember Gopāla and get involved in the development of this place, along with performing śravaṇa and kīrtana there?

There is a strong desire in the heart of this servant — once an ISKCON centre is opened at Bhubaneswar, this servant desires to stay there permanently, and then, to establish Gopāla's place as one of its branch centers. Oh Lord! Kindly shower Your mercy and make this successful.

### [GGS] closing refrain

Oh my Lord, Gopāla! Please shower Your mercy. May this servant achieve Kṛṣṇa-prema along with proper devotee association, well-wishers and companions. May this servant achieve success in the service of Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga.

## Entry 4 — 08–09 July 1975 · Puri, on the eve of Ratha-yātrā

- **Date:** `08-07-1975 to 09-07-1975` → `spoken_range(8,7,9,7,1975)` → "July eighth to ninth, nineteen seventy five."
- **Narrator intro:** none · **Closes with:** Gopāla refrain **+ [Haribol]**

### [GGS] diary body

By the mercy of Gopāla, somehow these two days passed in Puri. Tomorrow is Ratha-yātrā. Our stay at Puri has come to an end. Now our proposed plan is to return to Hyderabad because the temple president is repeatedly sending us letters to return after the Ratha-yātrā. By his blessings, somehow this servant was able to come to Odisha, as well as print and distribute a magazine in Odia. But O Lord! There is one thing for which I feel regret in my heart. One devotee had promised to come and take me to my dear Gopāla's place.

I had a desire to stay there for one day and perform bhajana-kīrtana along with preaching Śrī Guru's teachings to the local devotees. This was the special desire of this servant. But O Lord! What is Your desire? Why was he unable to come? Not only that, even after sending a letter to him is not responding. Why did we not receive any answer from the devotees at Gopāla's place? Only You know what the real fact is. May Your auspicious desire be fulfilled. This servant only desires that You kindly give him the association of real friends, well-wishers and associates, so that this servant may perform Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga's service for Their pleasure.

Śrī Guru has instructed this servant to preach Śrī Guru Gaurāṅga's message by opening a centre and to translate his books to Odia. How this will be possible is beyond this servant's imagination. O Lord! May Your auspicious desire be fulfilled.

### [GGS] closing refrain

Oh my Lord, Gopāla! Please shower Your mercy. May this servant achieve Kṛṣṇa-prema along with proper devotee association, well-wishers and companions. May this servant achieve success in the service of Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga. [Haribol]
