# Entry 3 — 01 June 1975 · "just like a machine in Your hands"


**[NARRATOR] (not his voice)** _(base-model male narrator — not his voice)_
1. Once again, on the following day, the remembrance of Gopāla's place awakened in the heart of His Holiness Gaur Govinda Swāmī Mahārāja.
2. How he offered heartfelt prayers at the lotus feet of the Lord to fulfill that divine longing, can be seen from his diary as follows.

**[GGS] diary body** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. Oh Lord… what You are doing — only You know.
2. This servant is unable to understand anything.
3. This servant is just like a machine in Your hands.
4. What You want to accomplish through this servant, You know it well.
5. Whatever is Your plan for this servant, You know it very well.
6. Anyhow, this servant has a strong desire, You know about his Gopāla's place.
7. This servant has developed a desire to open a branch of ISKCON at Gadeigiri, the place of Gopāla, to teach and preach about the Bhagavad-gītā to the devotees present there.
8. At present, Gopāla's place is in a very wretched condition.
9. How can we develop this place?
10. People in general are forgetting Gopāla, how can they once again remember Gopāla and get involved in the development of this place, along with performing śravaṇa and kīrtana there?
11. There is a strong desire in the heart of this servant — once an ISKCON centre is opened at Bhubaneswar, this servant desires to stay there permanently, and then, to establish Gopāla's place as one of its branch centers.
12. Oh Lord… Kindly shower Your mercy and make this successful.

**[GGS] closing refrain** _(his clone, calm ref)_
1. Oh my Lord, Gopāla… please shower Your mercy.
2. May this servant achieve Kṛṣṇa-prema along with proper devotee association, well-wishers and companions.
3. May this servant achieve success in the service of Śrī Śrī Guru and Gaurāṅga.
